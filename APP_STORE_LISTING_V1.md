**Description**

Make sense of your money, one decision at a time.

EquityClimb brings everyday financial planning into one straightforward place. Understand where your money goes, explore home ownership costs and see how your choices fit together.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
- Organize your budget, savings and financial goals.
- Build a clearer picture of your net worth.
- Explore mortgage payments, rate changes and home ownership costs using adjustable assumptions.
- Save properties you enter and compare their estimated costs.
- Learn financial concepts with an AI-powered guide that explains them in plain language.
- Check available Canadian rate and market context with source information.

PLAN AT YOUR OWN PACE
Start without signing in. You can choose to sign in later to sync supported information across devices.

Designed for people who want helpful explanations, transparent assumptions and a better way to plan their next financial step.

Important: EquityClimb is a financial education and planning tool. Calculations are estimates based on user inputs, not financial advice, mortgage pre-approval, investment recommendations or verified account balances. AI responses may be incorrect. Rates, market quotes and news may be delayed or unavailable; verify details with original sources and qualified professionals.

## Google Play (English — Canada)

**App name:** EquityClimb: Home & Money  
**Category:** Finance  
**Short description:** Budget, track net worth and explore Canadian home costs in one place.

**Full description**

Your money decisions deserve a clearer view.

EquityClimb is a Canadian financial planning companion for everyday budgets, home-buying decisions, savings goals and money education.

See the bigger picture:
• Organize spending, income, savings and goals.
• Review the financial details you enter in one place.
• Explore mortgage payments, interest-rate scenarios and estimated ownership costs.
• Save homes yourself and compare estimated monthly and upfront costs.
• Get plain-language explanations of financial concepts from AI Copilot.
• View available financial rates and market context with source details.

You're in control:
• Start with a guest experience.
• Choose whether to use supported cloud sync.
• Adjust assumptions without applying for a loan or placing a trade.
• Check how changing rates can affect an illustrative mortgage scenario.

EquityClimb is for learning and planning. It does not execute trades, connect to your bank accounts, issue mortgage approvals or guarantee any outcome. Calculations are estimates, AI may make mistakes, and rate and market data may be delayed. Independently verify important financial information before making decisions.
