Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

EquityClimb is a free personal-finance education and planning app. This page explains what information we use and why, in plain language.

Using the app without signing in

Everything you enter (income, budget, mortgage, savings, goals and Copilot chats) is saved only in your own browser on your device. We do not receive or store it.

Signing in (optional)

You can sign in with Google, with an emailed sign-in link, or (where available) with a text-message code to your phone. Sign-in is handled by Google Firebase Authentication. We receive only your email address or phone number, your name if your Google account shares it, and a private account ID. We never see a password.

We use this only to keep your numbers in sync between your devices. Your synced data is encrypted before it is stored on Cloudflare’s servers and is linked only to your account. We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

AI Copilot

When you ask Copilot a question, your question is sent to Cloudflare Workers AI to create an answer. If you tick “Use my numbers”, a summary of your figures is included so the answer can be personal. Untick it to keep your numbers out of AI requests.

News, rates and location

Live rates come from the Bank of Canada and news from public sources. Your approximate city may be detected from your internet connection to show local news. We do not use GPS.

Homes, searches and financial comparisons

Homes lets you enter and save property details and links in your financial plan. These manual entries are saved locally, and can also be included in your optional encrypted cloud sync. If you search for homes using a future authorized listings connection, your chosen city and search filters are sent to EquityClimb's Cloudflare endpoint and then to the licensed listing provider. A separate listing provider may process search data under its own terms. The app does not use your precise GPS location for Homes; you can choose your city yourself. Opening an external listing site is subject to that site's privacy policies.

Mortgage comparisons use the financial information already entered in EquityClimb. The app does not know whether an external listing is still available until a licensed provider supplies a current result.

Deleting your data

On your device: Settings → Erase everything. In your account: Settings → Delete cloud copy. You can also sign out at any time. To have your sign-in account removed completely, email us.

Not financial advice

EquityClimb provides general education and estimates only. Confirm important decisions with a licensed professional.
