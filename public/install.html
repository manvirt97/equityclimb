Open Equity Climb

Already installed. Open Equity Climb from your Home Screen.

iPhone and iPad

  1. Open equityclimb.pages.dev in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share icon.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  4. If shown, enable Open as Web App, then tap Add.

Android

  1. Open equityclimb.pages.dev in Chrome.
  2. Tap the three-dot menu.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  4. Confirm the installation.
Already using Equity Climb? If you've saved financial information as a guest, export a backup in Settings before installing or switching devices. Safari and the iPhone Home Screen app may use separate storage. Do not remove an existing installation until your data is backed up.
