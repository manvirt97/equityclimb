We're here to help.

Answers to common EquityClimb questions, without the jargon.

Sign-in or sync problem?

Check your internet connection, use the same sign-in method and account across devices, and try Settings → Sync now. If an email sign-in link has expired, request a new one. Never share sign-in links or session tokens with anyone.

Missing information after an update?

Don't clear your browser data or uninstall the home-screen app. Your device may still have your saved entries. Open Settings and check your available export or backup options before troubleshooting further.

Questions about financial estimates?

EquityClimb calculations are illustrative. Mortgage and budget results depend on the assumptions you enter. Market quotes and interest rates may be delayed or unavailable; check displayed source dates before making a decision.

Privacy or account help

Read our privacy policy for data handling and existing deletion options. Contact support to report a privacy concern or to inquire about full account deletion.

Contact support
